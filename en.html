How we work on your computer

  1. We check the computer

    We test the drive, memory and temperature, and tell you what is wrong before we touch anything.

  2. We repair or optimize the computer

    With your OK we replace parts, clean it and bring the system up to date.

  3. We return it with a report

    You know what was replaced, what was cleaned and what to check on the next visit.

How much does a diagnosis cost?

It depends on the device and the problem. We give you the price before we start and do nothing without your OK.

Can you help me choose a computer to buy?

Yes. We recommend the right computer for your work before you buy.

Will I lose my files during the repair?

No. We back up your files before replacing the drive or reinstalling the system.
